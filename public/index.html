Size Guide

General sizing for secondhand rackets and paddles across our four sports. These are typical ranges to help you compare listings — always check a specific model's spec if you can, since brands vary.

Tennis

Adult length27–27.5 in (68.5–70 cm)
Grip size4" – 4⅝" (L0–L5)
Head sizeMid 85–97 in² · Oversize 98–115 in²
Weight (unstrung)250g – 330g
Junior lengths19"–26" by age, before stepping up to 27" adult

Padel

LengthFixed at max 45.5 cm (all adult rackets)
WidthMax 26 cm
Weight350g – 385g
ShapeRound (control) · Teardrop (all-round) · Diamond (power)

Pickleball

Length + widthCombined max 24 in (typically ~15.5–16.5" × 7–8.25")
Grip circumference4" – 4.5"
WeightLight <7.3oz · Mid 7.3–8.4oz · Heavy >8.4oz

Squash

Max length27 in (68.6 cm)
Head size~500 cm² max, roughly 215mm × 184mm
Weight (unstrung)110g – 150g
GripOne general size — fine-tuned with grip wrap thickness

Buying secondhand? Ask the seller for the exact model name — most brands list precise specs online, which is the most reliable way to confirm fit.
